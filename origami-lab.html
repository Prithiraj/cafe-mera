Motion language study

The page should open, not float.

Three isolated prototypes for a futuristic, warm Cafe Mera interface: an architectural opening, a layered menu folio, and one connected photographic wall. No particles. No floating cards. No camera tricks.

Images no longer float independently. Three real Cafe Mera images are physically connected by shared hinges and unfold as one object.
