Meet Yeshi.

An Ethiopia native and long-time Colorado resident, Yeshi brings more than 25 years of hospitality experience to Cafe Mera.

The cafe's own story centers Ethiopian culture, quality coffee, delicious food and warm hospitality. That personal welcome is also one of the most consistent themes in customer reviews.
